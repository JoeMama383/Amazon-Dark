Probe-backed root cause
-----------------------
The v7.420 FULL probe on checkout "Need anything else? / Buy again" shows Amazon mounted the alternate speed-byg-sf-mobile-carousel renderer rather than the older dense-grid BYG renderer that the existing control selector expected.

Probe evidence:
- outer class: _speed-byg-sf-mobile-carousel_style_carouselContainer__2CmIF
- outer carousel background: rgb(255,255,255)
- add-to-cart wrapper: _speed-byg-sf-mobile-carousel_PlusButtonAtc-style_auiStepperSpotAtcButton__36opy
- actual control: button[name='submit.addToCart']
- actual control geometry: 32x32
- actual control background: rgb(255,216,20)
- plus glyph: .a-icon-small-add

Fix
---
1. Add only the speed-byg-sf-mobile-carousel carousel shell to the existing checkout BYG OLED floor owner.
2. Replace the dense-grid-only ATC selector with the stable semantic button[name='submit.addToCart'] selector under checkout-byg-mobile-container.
3. Preserve the stock circular geometry while applying #303335 fill, #747a7c border, light/white plus glyph, and #202324 pressed/focus fill.
4. Generalize existing BYG stepper styling from the old dense-grid wrapper to checkout-byg-mobile-container so both renderer variants keep dark controls.
5. Retain the old dense-grid ATC overlay transparency exception so product images cannot regain the historical black horizontal cut.
6. Preserve product images and authored semantic colors.
